## Banner Submission Guide

Want to see your server's branding in the archive? Follow these steps to ensure your banner is compatible with classic and modern Hotline clients.

### Technical Requirements

To maintain compatibility with Transaction 122 (Get Server Banner), all submissions must meet these specs:

- **Dimensions:** Exactly **468 x 60** pixels (Standard) or **600 x 60** pixels (Wide).
- **Format:** JPEG (.jpg) is preferred. Baseline (Standard) encoding only.
  - *Note: Do not use "Progressive" JPEGs; they appear as white boxes on 68k/PPC Macs.*
- **File Size:** Under **50KB**. Large banners cause lag during the login handshake.
- **Transparency:** Hotline does not support transparency in JPEGs. Use a solid background color (Black \#000000 is recommended).

### How to Submit

Currently, submissions are handled manually. Please provide the following:

1.  **The Image File:** Attach your .jpg or .gif.
2.  **Server Name:** The name of the server the banner belongs to.
3.  **Historical Context:** (Optional) Years the server was active and the primary focus (Files, Chat, Art).

### Submission Template

Copy and paste this when sending your request: `Server Name: [Name] Active Years: [e.g. 1998-2004] Banner Type: [Standard / Wide] Additional Info: [Your notes here]`
